challenge: Replace a legacy planning tool for a multi-stage dip-coating system in which carriers must satisfy different dwell times without crossing or blocking one another.
approach:
  - Translated equipment behavior, dwell-time requirements, carrier logic, and collision avoidance into a simulation and optimization model.
  - Used Differential Evolution to search for efficient movement strategies and compared results with the existing planning tool.
  - Added graphical carrier-path visualization to make candidate schedules reviewable by engineers.
outcomes:
  - Delivered an early-stage prototype with results comparable to the previous planning tool before the wider project was paused.
  - Established a basis for future cycle-time studies, carrier sizing, and optimization analysis.
