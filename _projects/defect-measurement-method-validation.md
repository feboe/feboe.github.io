challenge: Determine which defect-measurement method performs reliably across normal conditions and edge cases.

  - Built controlled test pieces with simulated defects of different sizes on components.
  - Compared 2D-to-3D regression, raycasting, and existing internal measurement methods.
  - Developed Streamlit analysis tools to visualize results, defect geometry, and special cases.
  - Documented method strengths, weaknesses, and selection logic for different use cases.

  - Established a preferred measurement method for future defect measurement.
  - Produced reusable Streamlit tools for geometry analysis and method comparison.
  - Documented decision logic for normal cases and edge conditions.
